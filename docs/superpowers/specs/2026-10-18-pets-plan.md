# Pets: raise a baby creature into your next avatar

Date: 2026-10-18. Status: building.

## The idea

Every island zone has a pet egg. Learning in that zone hatches the egg and grows
the pet through three stages: **Baby → Kid → Adult**. A grown-up pet becomes an
avatar you can play as. This replaces buying creatures with coins. Coins still
buy furniture and accessories.

## Decisions (from the owner)

- **Zone pets.** One pet per zone, and it grows with the zone's progress.
- **Coin shop replaced.** New avatars come only from raising pets. The starter
  creature (it matches the emoji picked at sign-up) and any creature already
  bought stay yours.
- **Taller adults.** Today's chibi creatures become the **Kid** stage.
  - Babies are smaller and rounder.
  - Adults are new, taller models with full features.
- **Pets appear in three places:**
  - toddling around the Home room;
  - one chosen "buddy" following you in the World;
  - a Pets page with all 12 pets.

## Growth rules

The stage is **derived** from progress by a pure function and is never stored.
`stars` is the zone's earned stars (each stage capped at its `starsToEarn`).
`total` is the zone's maximum.

| Stage | When |
|---|---|
| Egg | Nothing yet. |
| Baby | The zone's lesson is finished, **or** the zone has its first star. |
| Kid | `stars >= min(5, total - 1)`. That is 5 stars in most zones and 2 in Tower Battlefront, which has 3 stars in all. |
| Adult | `stars >= total`: the zone is complete, the same test as the zone celebration. Its creature joins your avatars. |

## Data (store `summerquest-progress-v1`, persist version 2 → 3)

- **`lessonsDone: Record<lessonId, number>`:** when each lesson was first
  finished. It is set when the lesson-complete card shows.
- **`petsSeen: Record<creatureId, 'egg' | PetStage>`:** the stage the child last
  saw celebrated. A pet whose derived stage is ahead of it has a celebration
  waiting.
- **`buddyPet: string | null`:** the pet that follows you in the World.
  - `null` means none chosen. The first pet to hatch becomes the buddy
    automatically.
  - The child can change the buddy, or leave it at Home, on the Pets page.
- **`ownedCreatures`:** unchanged. Adult pets are added to it, and the avatar
  picker lists them.
- **Migration from v2:**
  - Keep `ownedCreatures` and `activeCreature`.
  - Start `lessonsDone` empty.
  - Start `petsSeen` at each pet's current derived stage, so a player with lots
    of progress isn't met by a dozen celebrations at once.
  - Add any adult pets to `ownedCreatures`.

## Where pets show up

- **Pets page (`/pets`).**
  - All 12 pets, each as egg, baby, kid or adult.
  - What each needs next, e.g. "3 more ⭐ in Multiplication Mesa to grow up!",
    with a button to that zone.
  - The selected pet is shown in 3D: its stages side by side, with stages not
    yet reached as dark silhouettes.
  - Actions: make it the World buddy; for adults, **Become**.
  - Reached from the World HUD, the Home HUD and the island map.
- **Home room.**
  - Hatched pets toddle around: up to 6, the buddy first, and never the
    creature you're playing as.
  - Tap a pet for its card.
- **World buddy.**
  - The buddy pet trots after you, keeping about 1.2 m behind.
  - If it falls more than about 6 m behind, it pops back next to you.
  - It is not drawn while you're inside a building.
- **Celebrations.**
  - Hatching (the egg wobbles and cracks, and the baby pops out) and growing
    (sparkles, then the new stage).
  - Shown at calm moments: the World, the map, a zone, Home, Pets, or the
    lesson-complete card. Never in the middle of a game.
  - Several at once are queued, or summarized if there are more than 3.
  - An adult's celebration offers **"Become a Lion"** or "Not now".
- **Coin shop.**
  - Home's Creatures tab becomes "Animals":
    - your adults, with Become;
    - the others, showing their pet's progress and a link to Pets.
  - No more creature prices.

## Models (`src/home/models/creatures/*.tsx`)

Every creature component takes `{ stage?: 'baby' | 'kid' | 'adult' }` and
defaults to `'adult'` (avatars). `Staged` in `parts.tsx` picks the model:

```tsx
export function Fox({ stage }: CreatureProps) {
  return <Staged stage={stage} baby={FoxBaby} kid={FoxKid} adult={FoxAdult} />
}
```

A stage without art falls back to the kid model, shown at 60% size for a baby.

**Pets walk on their own.** The limb parts (`Leg`, `Arm`, `Wing`, `Wag`,
`Tentacle`) read the walk signal from `WalkStateContext`. Wrap a pet in
`<WalkStateContext.Provider value={petWalk}>` and drive `petWalk` yourself (or
use `useWanderWalk({ walk: petWalk, steer: false })`). Otherwise it walks in step
with the player.

**Review:**
- `/home?studio=growth` shows every creature as baby, kid and adult next to a
  height ruler (red ticks at 0.5, 1.0 and 1.5).
- `/home?studio=growth&only=lion` shows just one.
- `cam=x,y,z` and `target=x,y,z` set the camera.

### Art direction

All three stages are the **same character** at three ages. They share the
palette, face style (eyes, blush, smile), ink lines and plush-toy feel. Nothing
is scary: no fangs, no claws, no angry brows.

| | Baby | Kid (today's model) | Adult |
|---|---|---|---|
| Height to top of head | about 0.5–0.55 | about 0.93 | **about 1.25–1.35** (ears, horns and crests may reach about 1.65) |
| Head share of height | about 65% | about 55% | about 38–42% |
| Legs | stubby nubs, hip about 0.07–0.09 | hip 0.17 | long, hip about 0.42–0.5 |
| Body | a round bean | round | longer torso with a hint of a neck |
| Eyes | biggest relative to the head, set low | as now | a little smaller relative to the head, still big and friendly |
| Signature features | nubs and tufts | as now (may be toned down slightly to sit between) | **in full** |
| Draw-call budget (shapes, not counting `Ink`) | ≤ 22 | as now | ≤ 40 |

**Signature features, by creature:**
- **Lion:** baby has a tuft of hair; adult a big layered mane and a tail tuft.
- **Dragon / dragonet:** baby has wing nubs; adult proper wings, horns, back
  spines and a long tail.
- **Unicorn:** baby has a nub horn; adult a long spiral horn, a flowing rainbow
  mane and tail, and hooves.
- **Owl:** baby is a downy puff; adult is tall, with ear tufts and layered wing
  feathers.
- **Frog:** baby is a froglet with a little tadpole tail; adult has long legs and
  a wide smile.
- **Octopus:** baby is tiny with stubby tentacles; adult has a tall mantle and
  long, curling tentacles.
- **T-Rex:** baby wears an eggshell cap; adult is tall, with the iconic tiny arms
  and a long counterweight tail.
- **Tiger:** adult has bold stripes and a long striped tail.
- **Fox:** adult has a longer snout and a big bushy tail.
- **Bear / panda:** adults are big, sturdy and soft.

**Rules for adults (they are avatars):**
- Keep the walkable footprint within a 0.3 radius around the feet. Tails and
  wings may reach out behind.
- Update the adult's row in `src/home/models/anchors.ts` so every accessory
  still fits. Check with `/home?studio=anchors` (all creatures) and
  `/home?studio=accgrid` (all 16 accessories on the bear).
- Faces point +z, and feet stand on y = 0.
